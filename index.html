Thin-Film Device · Sep – Oct 2024
DSSC Fabrication & SnO₂ Thin-Film Electrical Characterization
+
Assembled DSSC cell
Live PV output — 0.174 VDC
SnO₂-coated glass — VDP probe contacts
SEM/EDS — Sn & O elemental analysis
UV-Vis transmittance (250–500 nm) — 0, 3, 6, 9 coating layers

Fabricated functional dye-sensitized solar cells (DSSC) — a complete wet thin-film photovoltaic device — and ran a parallel optimization study on SnO₂ transparent conductive coatings via spray deposition. Used VDP 4-point probe, UV-Vis spectroscopy, and SEM/EDS to determine the optimal 6-layer coating configuration and quantify the thickness–transparency trade-off.

94–137 mVPV output across 3 conditions
~3,925 ΩOptimal sheet resistance (6L)
0.56 kΩMin substrate resistance
6 layersOptimal SnO₂ config
Spray DepositionVDP 4-Point Probe SEM / EDSUV-Vis Spectroscopy MATLABRoot Cause Analysis
  • Fabricated full DSSC stack: TiO₂ film, raspberry dye sensitizer, iodine electrolyte, graphite film on FTO glass
  • Deposited SnO₂ across 3-, 6-, 9-layer configs via spray deposition; 6-coat identified as optimal via VDP probe
  • UV-Vis (250–500 nm) quantified inverse relationship between coating thickness and transmittance
  • SEM/EDS verified surface morphology and elemental composition: 98.1% O, 1.9% Sn atomic conc.
  • Applied root cause analysis to resolve VDP measurement anomalies via contact preparation correction
